Q: Walk me through what happens when a customer's payment fails.

A: So today, when a payment fails, the order just sits in a "needs review"
state. Someone on my team — we call that the Order Desk Clerk role,
it's not a dedicated job title, just whoever's covering the queue that
day — has to go in and manually decide whether to retry the charge or
contact the customer. It's not automated at all right now.

Q: How often does that happen?

A: A handful of times a day. Not huge volume, but it's manual toil we'd
like to get rid of once the new Payment Processing service is live.

Q: Anything else about the order flow worth knowing?

A: Just that once payment goes through, the handoff to shipping is
automatic — that part already works fine and isn't in scope for this
project.
